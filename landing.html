Example deal · Sheffield 3-bed (illustrative, not a real listing)

Maximum bid to hit a 7% gross yield (BTL)

£162,900

Asking £175,000 · Rent £950/mo · £950 × 12 ÷ 7% = £162,857

BRRR£36,403left in deal
BTL7.0%gross yield
HMO10.5%gross yield
SA12.8%gross yield

All at the same £162,900 price. BRRR assumes a £20,000 refurb, £210,000 end value and a 75% refinance, with stamp duty and fees included; BTL at £950/mo rent; HMO assumes 3 room lets at £475/mo each; SA assumes a short-let at £95/night, 60% occupancy.
