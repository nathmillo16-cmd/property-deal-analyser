The refinance date is the fixed rate end date (or a BRRR property's refinance date). If it has already passed, the planner uses today. You can pick an earlier date for any property: before the fix ends, an early repayment charge (a % of the balance) is taken off too. Repayment balances are worked out month by month at today's rate over your remaining term (25 years if not entered). When nothing can be released, the new payment is shown on today's balance, as a straight remortgage. Rent and running costs stay at today's figures.

A planning guide based on the figures in your Portfolio, not financial advice. Lender criteria, valuations and rates vary: speak to a broker before you refinance.
