Questions by section

HomeYour dashboard and what needs attention

What does Home show?

Your deals analysed, what's in your pipeline, recent saved deals and anything that needs a nudge, such as an offer waiting for a reply.

What is Quick analyse?

Type a price and a rent for a fast yield and a rough maximum bid. Open it in the full calculator when you want the detail.

AnalyseThe deal calculator: BTL, BRRR, HMO, SA and Flip

Which figures do I need?

Purchase price, end value and rent (or nightly rate for SA, or sale value for Flip). Everything else has a sensible starting figure you can change.

What is the maximum bid?

The most you can pay and still hit your target yield or ROI. Set your targets on the calculator or in Settings.

Why are there Cash, Interest-only, Repayment and BRRR tabs?

They are the different ways to pay for the same deal. Flick between them to see how each changes your cashflow and return.

Is stamp duty included?

Yes, at additional property rates for England and Northern Ireland. It counts in your total investment and ROI. The Stamp duty calculator in Tools shows the band breakdown.

DealsSaving deals, the board and the deal workspace

How do I save a deal?

Press Save deal under the results and give it a name or address. Free accounts can save 2 deals; paid accounts can save as many as they like.

What is the board?

Your pipeline. Move deals from Analysing to Viewing, Offered, Agreed and Completed, by dragging or with the stage menu.

Where do notes and offers go?

Open any saved deal to see its workspace: the figures, comps, seller position, notes, calls, viewings and offers, all in one place.

PortfolioProperties you own, rent and costs

How do I add a property?

Add it on the Portfolio page, or open a saved deal and choose Move to portfolio once you've bought it.

Can I get figures for my accountant?

Yes. Log rent and expenses on each property, then download the tax year summary as a PDF or CSV.

ToolsCalculators, refurb costs and letters

What are the calculators for?

Quick answers to one question each: stamp duty, a BRRR refinance, what a lender will lend on the rent, bridging costs, and owning personally or through a company. Run a full analysis from any of them to carry the figures into the main calculator.

How do I cost a refurb?

Use the Refurb Estimator, line by line or trade by trade. Save it and link it to a deal to update that deal's figures.

What are Letter Templates?

Ready-made letters for empty properties, probate, follow-ups and direct offers. Fill in the details, then copy or download a PDF.

Plan & accountWhat's included, billing and your details

What does the paid plan add?

Unlimited saved deals, the deal board, Portfolio, the Refurb Estimator, Letter Templates, comps and area data, and the AI verdict.

Where is my plan?

Plan & billing in the account menu shows your current plan.

How do I change my email or password?

In Settings. Forgotten your password? Use Forgot password on the log in page.
