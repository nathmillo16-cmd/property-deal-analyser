Contact us

Email cameron@propulsionproperty.co.uk any time. We aim to reply within 2 working days. [CHECK] confirm the response time you want to promise.

If you're logged in, you can also use the Contact us form. On the website it's at the top of every page, next to your account menu. In the iPhone app, tap More, then Contact us. The form lets you pick a category (bug, question, feature idea or billing), describe what happened and attach a screenshot, which helps us fix problems faster.

Reset your password

If you've forgotten it:

  1. Go to the Log in page and tap Forgot password?
  2. Enter the email address you signed up with and tap Send reset email.
  3. Open the email and tap the link. Check your spam folder if it hasn't arrived after a few minutes.
  4. Choose a new password. You'll be logged straight in.

If you're logged in and want to change it: go to Settings (in the app: More, then Account & settings), find Account, enter your current password and your new one, and save.

Delete your account

  1. Log in and open Settings. In the app, tap More, then Account & settings.
  2. Scroll to the Danger zone section at the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  3. Type your account's email address to confirm, then tap Permanently delete my account.

This is permanent and can't be undone. It removes your login and everything tied to it: saved deals, pipeline data, notes, offers, portfolio, refurb estimates and support requests. If you have a paid plan, it is cancelled first.

Can't log in? Email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you. See our privacy policy for what we keep and why.

Frequently asked questions

I didn't get my confirmation email

Check your spam or junk folder first. If it's not there, try logging in with your email and password. If your email isn't confirmed yet, a "Resend confirmation email" option will appear.

Is the analysis financial advice?

No. Every figure is an estimate based on the numbers and assumptions you enter. Always check a deal with a broker, surveyor or accountant before acting on it.

Where do the comparable sales come from?

Sold prices come from HM Land Registry's Price Paid Data, within half a mile of the postcode you search. Floor areas come from the government's EPC register. Crime, planning and employment figures come from Police UK, planning.data.gov.uk and the ONS.

Why does the Article 4 row say to check with the council?

The government's Article 4 dataset doesn't cover every council yet, so "none recorded" doesn't guarantee there isn't one. If you're planning an HMO, always confirm with the local council.

Can I use PROPulsion on my phone?

Yes. The website works in your phone's browser, and there's also an iPhone app. Your saved deals are the same everywhere you log in.

My deal reminders aren't showing (iPhone app)

Reminders need notifications turned on. Open your iPhone's Settings, tap Notifications, find Propulsion Property and allow notifications. Reminders are stored on your phone only, so they won't appear on another device.

Is my data shared or sold?

We never sell your data. We only share it with the services we need to run the app. Our privacy policy lists exactly what we collect and where it goes.
