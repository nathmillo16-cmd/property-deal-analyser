How this is worked outInterest, repayments and the tax year
Simple interestprincipal owed x rate x days / 365
Compound interestthe same, and on each anniversary of the start date unpaid interest is added to the balance
Each repaymentpays interest built up first, then principal
Owed todayprincipal owed + unpaid interest

Interest builds up day by day from the start date, as simple interest on the balance at the start of each loan year (less any repayments since). With compound interest, interest still unpaid on each anniversary of the start date is added to the balance, so the next year's interest is charged on it too. In the first year, compound and simple are the same. "Interest this tax year" is the interest built up since 6 April, paid or not. If you pay interest monthly, log each payment so the balance stays right. Repayments due within 30 days, or overdue, show on Home under "Needs attention".

A record to help you keep track, not legal or tax advice. Your signed loan agreement is what counts: check figures against it.
